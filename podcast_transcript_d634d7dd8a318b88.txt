[Speaker: Matt Perault]
But the consumer welfare standard is actually broader than that. And it actually also includes harm to quality, harm to innovation. Like, if Yelp is frustrated with Google, Spotify is frustrated with Apple, instead of focusing on how competitors fare, but rather how consumers fare: Are they getting better services?
