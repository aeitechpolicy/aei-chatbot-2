	Brookings—AEI Commission on U.S. Rural Prosperity | A conversation with John Roy Price | American Enterprise Institute - AEI

John Roy Price, historian advisor to the Brookings-AEI Commission on US Rural Prosperity and former special assistant to President Nixon, joins Brent Orrell (AEI) and Tony Pipa (Brookings) to discuss the history of federal rural policy. Price, author of “The Last Liberal Republican,” reflects on his firsthand role in shaping Nixon’s domestic agenda, including the Rural Affairs Council, the growth center concept, and the Family Assistance Plan.
The conversation explores how urban and rural policy were seen as deeply interconnected in the early 1970s, why Nixon’s ambitious rural development and welfare reform proposals ultimately failed, and how the challenges of that era — out-migration, poverty, fragmented federal programs, and the tension between federal control and local authority — mirror the rural policy debates we face today.
