	AI and the American Workforce | American Enterprise Institute - AEI

In this episode, AEI Senior Fellow Brent Orrell joins the Reagan Caucus to explore how artificial intelligence is reshaping the American workforce. From the deskilling of knowledge jobs to the challenges facing recent college grads, Orrell unpacks the economic, educational, and policy implications of rapid AI adoption. The conversation covers the need for future-focused workforce planning, the enduring value of human and social skills, and why smarter—not more—regulation is key to managing AI’s impact.
