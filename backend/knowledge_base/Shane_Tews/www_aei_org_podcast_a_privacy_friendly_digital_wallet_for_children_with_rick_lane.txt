	A Privacy-Friendly Digital Wallet for Children (with Rick Lane)

[Speaker: Introduction / editorial context]
How can children enjoy the groundbreaking innovations of the digital age with sufficient guardrails around their personal data? What existing laws and regulations aim to protect children online, and what steps must businesses of all sizes take to comply with them?

[Speaker: Introduction / editorial context]
On this episode of “Explain to Shane,” Shane sits down with Rick Lane, founder and CEO of Iggy Ventures, to discuss why his firm chose to advise and invest in Rego Digital Architectures’ Mazoola app. Mazoola is the only independently certified family mobile wallet that is compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act of 1998 and Europe’s General Data Protection Regulation.
