	America’s 5G Future: Risks and Rewards (with Tommy Ross)

[Speaker: Introduction / editorial context]
If the US rollout of 5G wireless is to reach its full potential, network security needs to be a priority. But what are the security risks of 5G, and how can the US win the global 5G race?

[Speaker: Introduction / editorial context]
On this episode, Shane sits down with Tommy Ross, senior policy director at BSA | The Software Alliance and author of BSA’s most recent position paper titled “Securing 5G: A Call to Harness Software Innovation,” which discusses the transition from hardware-dominated networking to a software-centric model for 5G. Together, Shane and Tommy address the availability and importance of 5G network security tools.
